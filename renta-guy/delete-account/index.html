You can delete your Renta-guy account and associated personal data at any time either directly inside the Android app (instant automated deletion) or from the web below without reinstalling or signing back into the app.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open Renta-guy on your Android device and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Delete Account in the top header bar, or open the Profile tab and scroll to Data Privacy, Keystore Security & Account Deletion.
  3. Type DELETE, check the confirmation box, and tap Permanently Delete My Account.
  4. Renta-guy immediately purges your mutable Firestore records, destroys your local Android Keystore AES-256-GCM keys, and deletes your Firebase Auth login.

Option 2: Web Request (No App Required)

  1. Send an email from the Google email address linked to your Renta-guy account using the button below (or write directly to help@abstrekkt.com).
  2. Specify whether you want Full Account & Data Deletion or Partial Data Deletion (for example, deleting uploaded BASSET/Food Handler credentials or Form W-4 records while keeping your login).
  3. We verify ownership via your sign-in email address, complete deletion within 7 business days, and email you a confirmation receipt.
