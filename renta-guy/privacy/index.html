Privacy Policy for Renta-guy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Android Package: com.aistudio.rentashifts.lkcty · Operator: Abstrekkt · Support: help@abstrekkt.com

Summary of Commitments: Renta-guy connects hospitality venues and front-of-house staff across Lake County, Illinois for urgent shift coverage and statutory W-2 payroll administration. We run zero advertising SDKs, never sell personal data, request zero raw camera or GPS location permissions in the Android manifest, and provide immediate self-service account deletion both inside the Android app and on our Account & Data Deletion page.

1. Categories of Data Collected and Purpose

Renta-guy collects only the data required to authenticate accounts, verify hospitality role permits, match shifts, and administer statutory payroll withholdings:

Data Category Specific Fields Collection Trigger & Purpose
Account Identity Full name or worker display alias, email address, Firebase Authentication UID Required when signing in via Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager) for account management and fraud prevention.
Contact & Municipality Optional phone number, home municipality (e.g., Libertyville), preferred travel radius, venue street address Used for shift eligibility matching and booking contact handoff once a shift is confirmed.
Tax & Payroll Records Legal name, residential mailing address, Federal Form W-4 / Illinois Form IL-W-4 elections, 9-digit SSN/ITIN (encrypted on-device), shift gross pay, tax withholdings, and net payout summaries Required only if completing W-2 worker tax onboarding or settling shifts under IRC Section 6109 and 26 CFR Section 31.6051-1.
Permits & Dispute Evidence Illinois BASSET certificate numbers, Food Handler credentials, issuing authority, expiration dates, SHA-256 digests, and optional permit or dispute images selected via Android Photo Picker Optional unless claiming alcohol-service or food-handling roles or submitting shift incident evidence.
Security & App Integrity Firebase Installation ID, Firebase App Check tokens (Play Integrity / reCAPTCHA Enterprise), IP security logs Required automatically by Google Firebase to block automated abuse and unauthorized database requests.

2. Android Permissions, On-Device Encryption & Gemini AI Processing

  • Minimal Android Manifest Permissions: Renta-guy declares only android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. The app does not request CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
  • Zero-Permission QR Arrival & Photo Selection: Venue arrival QR codes are scanned through Google Play Services Code Scanner (GmsBarcodeScanning), which returns only the decoded token string to Renta-guy without exposing camera frames. Optional credential images use the system Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia).
  • AES-256-GCM Tax ID Sealing: When a worker submits a 9-digit SSN or ITIN on Form W-4 / IL-W-4, Renta-guy encrypts the identifier on-device with Android Keystore AES-256-GCM (renta_w4_tin_aes256_key) before storage and displays only the truncated mask (***-**-XXXX) on profiles and Copy B statements.
  • Optional AI Assistance: When you invoke the shift planning assistant or run an optional permit photo legibility pre-check, the prompt text or selected permit image is sent over TLS to Google Gemini APIs solely to return real-time shift or legibility feedback.

3. Data Sharing and Service Providers

Renta-guy does not sell personal data or share user information with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed exclusively by infrastructure service providers acting on behalf of Renta-guy:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Gemini APIs): Hosts encrypted account, shift, credential, and audit records in the United States.
  • Stripe (Test / Settlement Infrastructure): Processes venue shift payment intents when live card settlement is enabled; Renta-guy does not store raw payment card numbers or bank routing numbers inside the Android app.
  • Counterparty Shift Disclosure: When a worker applies for or claims a venue's shift, role qualifications and booking contact details are disclosed to the authorized manager of that venue to fulfill the shift.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You may delete your Renta-guy account and mutable personal data at any time:

  • In-App Self-Service Deletion: Open Renta-guy and select Delete Account in the top bar, on the sign-in screen, or at the bottom of the Profile tab, type DELETE, and confirm. This immediately deletes your Firebase Auth login, RBAC profile, worker profile, Form W-4 encrypted SSN/ITIN record, credential cards, purged vault URIs, venue records, trust roster links, applications, notifications, unsettled shift drafts, and local Android Keystore keys.
  • Web / Out-of-App Deletion (No Reinstall Required): Visit our dedicated deletion page or email help@abstrekkt.com to request full account deletion or partial deletion of uploaded credentials without opening the app. Verified web/email deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.
  • Statutory Retention Exceptions: Where shifts have been worked or settled, Renta-guy retains de-coupled W-2 tax summaries and wage withholding records for 4 to 7 years as required by IRS regulations (26 CFR Section 31.6001-1) and Illinois tax law, and retains shift dispute and compliance audit logs for 3 years under the Illinois Minimum Wage Law (820 ILCS 105/8).

5. Age Restriction & Contact Information

Renta-guy is intended exclusively for adult hospitality workers and venue managers aged 18 and older (and 21+ where Illinois municipal alcohol-service ordinances require). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. For privacy inquiries, access requests, or deletion support, contact Abstrekkt at help@abstrekkt.com.
